## 摘要

To profile unknown ncRNAs-"dark matter" in single cells, we developed dropTotal, a high-throughput droplet-based total RNA-seq method that uses dU-modified GAT primer with temperature-ramp hybridization and droplet merge barcoding to co-detect coding and non-coding transcripts with record sensitivity (>13,500 genes/cell, including >2,000 lncRNAs and >500 sncRNAs), compatible with fresh, frozen, fixed, and FFPE tissues. Applied to ~75,000 human glioma nuclei, it captured 60,313 genes (18,681 lncRNA, 19,859 mRNAs and 6,753 sncRNAs), enabling ncRNA-driven regulatory landscape construction. In oligodendroglioma, module analysis identified recurrence-associated ncRNA-centered modules linked to therapy resistance and invasion; in glioblastoma, six cellular states showed hundreds of state-specific unannotated ncRNAs with divergent functions, from MIR222HG-mediated immune modulation to SCIRT-driven hypoxia adaptation. Alternative splicing analysis identified 428 state-specific junction markers and mapped cell-state-specific alternative splicing regulation. dropTotal offers broad application for decoding the underlying ncRNA biology and single-cell whole transcriptome regulatory mechanisms in cellular identity and disease progression.
