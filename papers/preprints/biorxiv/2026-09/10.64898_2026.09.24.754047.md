## 摘要

Thrombostasin is a cysteine-free anticoagulant protein produced by the horn fly Haematobia irritans, featuring an unusual stretch of seven consecutive aspartate residues within the polypeptide sequence. Here, we report the first chemical synthesis of native thrombostasin and a hepta-glutamate analogue, together with assessment of their thrombin inhibitory and anticoagulant activity.
