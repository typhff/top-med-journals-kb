## 摘要

The authors have withdrawn this manuscript because the sequenced individual was misidentified as Quercus canariensis following a label swap and was subsequently confirmed to be Quercus pyrenaica. A corrected manuscript focusing on Q. pyrenaica will be submitted shortly to avoid propagating the incorrect species attribution. Therefore, the authors do not wish this work to be cited as reference for the project. If you have any questions, please contact the corresponding author.
