## 摘要

Histological analysis of 3D tissue models is limited by geometric sampling bias arising from inconsistent positioning during embedding. We present Coplanar Organoid Reproducible Embedding (CORE), which aligns heterogeneous specimens on a shared sectioning plane to enable reproducible, bias-free histology. By eliminating depth-dependent sampling artifacts, CORE reveals biological differences obscured by sectioning depth and integrates with automated liquid- handling workflows.
