## 摘要

The authors have withdrawn their manuscript because a subsequent audit identified implementation errors in the analytical pipeline used for the preprint, affecting environmental-data ingestion and temporal feature construction, spatial grid registration, rank handling, and the treatment of temporal autocorrelation in pooled space-time analyses. These issues affect several numerical results and inferential statements in the posted version. A substantially revised analysis, developed from a separate analytical branch, was subsequently peer reviewed and published in Biology (2026), 15, 1415 (DOI: 10.3390/biology15161415), and represents the final analysis of the project. Because revisions can no longer be posted after journal publication, the authors are withdrawing the preprint rather than leaving the uncorrected version as the current record. Therefore, the authors do not wish this work to be cited as reference for the project. If you have any questions, please contact the corresponding author.
