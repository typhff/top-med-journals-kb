## 摘要

Cytos Multimodal Pooled Screening (CytosMPS) measures CRISPR guide identity, morphology, native 3' transcriptome, and protein abundance and localization in the same fixed cells. Following IL-1{beta} stimulation, cells with higher nuclear NF-{kappa}B show lower phospho-p38/HSP27 and higher NFKBIA/DUSP1 in the same cell, a measurement neither imaging nor transcriptomics alone provides. TGFBR2 and CTNNB1 perturbations produce distinct multimodal phenotypes. Guide representation and perturbation transcriptional responses agree with an orthogonal single-cell transcriptomic platform.
