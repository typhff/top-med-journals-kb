## 摘要

Evidence on vaccine effectiveness (VE) of 2025-26 COVID-19 vaccines is limited. We estimated VE of BNT162b2 LP.8.1-adapted vaccine among non-immunocompromised adults [&ge;]65 years through December 2025 using linked claims and immunization registry data from two U.S. states. VE against COVID-19-related ED/UC encounters was 48% (95% CI:19, 66).
