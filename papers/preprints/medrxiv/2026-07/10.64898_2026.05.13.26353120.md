## 摘要

Withdrawal StatementThe authors have withdrawn this manuscript because on re-evaluation of the genetic instrument employed for GLP1-R it became apparent that the instrument published in the literature is in fact not robust for application in drug-target Mendelian randomisation. It contains predominantly highly correlated single nucleotide polymorphisms, which violate a core MR assumption. Therefore, we are now re-generating the genetic instrument, using our own pipeline, rather than a published one. Therefore, the authors do not wish this work to be cited as reference for the project. If you have any questions, please contact the corresponding author.
