## 摘要

Sentinel lymph node biopsy (SLNB) is increasingly omitted in early-stage breast cancer, often prompting whole-breast irradiation (WBI). We evaluated partial-breast irradiation (PBI) without axillary surgery among 78 clinically node-negative patients (median age 75) treated from 2014-2022. After 53-month median follow-up, no ipsilateral, regional, or distant recurrences occurred. These results demonstrate excellent outcomes and suggest PBI is a feasible, safe alternative to WBI when SLNB is omitted.
